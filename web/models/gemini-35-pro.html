Gemini 3.5 Pro

business Maker: Google account_tree Family: Gemini 3

// Overview

Gemini 3.5 Pro is the flagship "Pro" tier of Google DeepMind's Gemini 3.5 family. Google introduced the family at Google I/O on May 19, 2026 under the banner "frontier intelligence with action," but on that day only Gemini 3.5 Flash actually shipped. For Gemini 3.5 Pro, Google's official blog said only: "We're also hard at work on 3.5 Pro. It's already being used internally, and we look forward to rolling it out next month." No specifications, benchmarks, pricing, or model ID were published.

As of June 18, 2026, Gemini 3.5 Pro still has not been generally released. It does not appear in the official Gemini API changelog or on Google's developer pricing page, both of which list Gemini 3.5 Flash (released May 19, 2026) and the older Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview, but no Gemini 3.5 Pro. The model has been described as in internal use and limited enterprise preview, with broad availability still pending sometime in June 2026.

Positioned above Gemini 3.5 Flash, Gemini 3.5 Pro is expected to be Google's top reasoning and multimodal model, succeeding Gemini 3.1 Pro and absorbing the heavy frontier workloads. Widely-circulated figures such as a 2-million-token context window, a "Deep Think" reasoning mode, and roughly $15/$60-per-million-token pricing come from third-party tech press and analyst speculation — Google has not confirmed any of them. Treat every number for Gemini 3.5 Pro as unconfirmed until Google publishes an official model card.

// FAQ

Has Gemini 3.5 Pro been released?

No. Gemini 3.5 Pro was announced at Google I/O on May 19, 2026, but only Gemini 3.5 Flash shipped that day. Google said Pro was "already being used internally" and would roll out "next month." As of June 18, 2026 it still has not been generally released and is not listed in the public Gemini API changelog or pricing page.

What is the difference between Gemini 3.5 Pro and Gemini 3.5 Flash?

Flash is the faster, cheaper, already-available tier of the Gemini 3.5 family (released May 19, 2026 at $1.50/$9 per million input/output tokens). Pro is meant to be the more capable flagship tier above Flash, aimed at the hardest reasoning, agentic, and long-context tasks — but it has not shipped, so its exact capabilities and price are not yet official.

What are Gemini 3.5 Pro's specs and pricing?

Google has not published official specs, benchmarks, a knowledge cutoff, or pricing for Gemini 3.5 Pro. Figures circulating online — such as a 2-million-token context window, a "Deep Think" mode, and roughly $15/$60 per million tokens — are third-party estimates, not confirmed by Google. For comparison, the shipped Gemini 3.5 Flash costs $1.50/$9 per million tokens and Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview costs $2/$12.

When will Gemini 3.5 Pro be available?

Google targeted "next month" from its May 19, 2026 announcement, pointing to a June 2026 launch, but it gave no specific date. The model has been in internal and limited enterprise preview. Until Google publishes an official model card and adds it to the Gemini API, the release date and details remain unconfirmed.
