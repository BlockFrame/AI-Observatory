GPT-5.2 Pro

business Maker: OpenAI account_tree Family: GPT-5

// Overview

GPT-5.2 Pro is the top reasoning tier of OpenAI's GPT-5.2 family, released on December 11, 2025. Inside ChatGPT, GPT-5.2 ships in three modes — Instant, Thinking, and Pro — where Pro spends the most test-time reasoning compute. OpenAI positions GPT-5.2 Pro as its smartest and most trustworthy model for difficult questions where a higher-quality answer is worth the wait, showing fewer major errors than GPT-5.2 Thinking across complex domains like programming and science.

Technically, GPT-5.2 Pro shares the GPT-5.2 family envelope: a 400,000-token context window, up to 128,000 output tokens, an August 31, 2025 knowledge cutoff, and text plus image (vision) input. It is exposed in the OpenAI API through the Responses API as the model id gpt-5.2-pro. The launch followed an internal 'Code Red' push, arriving roughly three weeks after Google's Gemini 3 Pro.

GPT-5.2 Pro briefly held the verified state-of-the-art on ARC-AGI-2: the ARC Prize team independently confirmed 54.2% for the Pro (High) setting, ahead of GPT-5.2 Thinking's 52.9%. OpenAI has since released GPT-5.4 and GPT-5.5, so GPT-5.2 Pro is now documented as a previous-generation model, but it remains a notable data point for how far extra reasoning compute pushed frontier accuracy in late 2025.

// FAQ

When was GPT-5.2 Pro released?

OpenAI released the GPT-5.2 family — Instant, Thinking, and Pro — on December 11, 2025, beginning with paid ChatGPT plans. GPT-5.2 Pro is the highest-effort reasoning tier of that family.

How is GPT-5.2 Pro different from GPT-5.2 Thinking?

Both are reasoning models, but Pro spends more test-time reasoning compute than Thinking. OpenAI positions it as the smartest, most trustworthy option for difficult questions where a higher-quality answer is worth waiting longer for, with fewer major errors on complex tasks.

What is GPT-5.2 Pro's context window and knowledge cutoff?

GPT-5.2 Pro shares the family envelope: a 400,000-token context window, up to 128,000 output tokens, and an August 31, 2025 knowledge cutoff. It accepts text and image (vision) input, per OpenAI's API documentation.

How good is GPT-5.2 Pro on ARC-AGI-2?

The ARC Prize team independently verified GPT-5.2 Pro (High) at 54.2% on ARC-AGI-2 — state-of-the-art at the time — ahead of GPT-5.2 Thinking's 52.9%, at a measured cost of about $15.72 per task.
