Qwen3.8-Max-Preview

business Maker: Alibaba (Qwen) account_tree Family: Qwen 3

// Overview

Qwen3.8-Max-Preview is the preview of the next flagship in Alibaba's Qwen-Max line, unveiled by the Qwen team on July 19, 2026 during the World Artificial Intelligence Conference (WAIC) in Shanghai. Alibaba describes it as a 2.4-trillion-parameter model and as its first Qwen model above one trillion parameters to accept images, video, and documents alongside text — making it a multimodal successor to the text-only Qwen3.7-Max.

The preview is available through the Alibaba Cloud Token Plan subscription and the company's Qoder and QoderWork agentic platforms, priced during the trial period at 10% of the standard rate. Alibaba has not published a benchmark table, a model card, an activated-parameter count, or a formal license; the Qwen team has said the model is "continuously evolving" and that open weights will follow, without committing to a date.

Alibaba's own positioning claim is that Qwen3.8-Max-Preview is second only to Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 among today's frontier systems. No third-party leaderboard (Artificial Analysis, LMArena, or otherwise) had independent scores for the model at launch, so treat every quality claim about Qwen3.8-Max as unverified until the Qwen team publishes an official benchmark table or an external evaluator reports numbers.

// FAQ

Has Qwen3.8-Max been released?

It is available as a preview, not a general release. The Qwen team unveiled Qwen3.8-Max-Preview on July 19, 2026 at the World Artificial Intelligence Conference in Shanghai, offered through the Alibaba Cloud Token Plan, Qoder, and QoderWork at 10% of standard pricing during the trial period. Alibaba has not published an official model card, benchmark table, or license.

What are Qwen3.8-Max's specs?

Alibaba has disclosed only that Qwen3.8-Max has 2.4 trillion total parameters and is its first Qwen model above one trillion parameters to accept images, video, and documents alongside text input. The active-parameter count, the context window, the training data, and the architecture have not been officially published. Third-party recaps have suggested a 1M-token context window, but this is not confirmed by the Qwen team.

Is Qwen3.8-Max open-source or open-weight?

Not yet. Qwen3.8-Max-Preview is a proprietary preview served through Alibaba's own platforms. The Qwen team has said that open weights will follow, but has not published a license or a release date.

How does Qwen3.8-Max compare to Claude Fable 5 or Kimi K3?

Alibaba's own positioning claim is that Qwen3.8-Max is second only to Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 among the systems the Qwen team benchmarked. Independent leaderboards (Artificial Analysis, LMArena) had no scores at launch, so this ranking is Alibaba's internal claim, not a verified third-party result. Moonshot's Kimi K3, previewed three days earlier at 2.8 trillion parameters, is the other headline preview in this weight class.
