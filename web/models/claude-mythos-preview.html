Claude Mythos Preview

business Maker: Anthropic account_tree Family: Claude Fable / Mythos

// Overview

Claude Mythos Preview is a general-purpose frontier model from Anthropic, the first member of its Claude Fable / Mythos (Mythos-class) line. Anthropic announced it on April 7, 2026, alongside Project Glasswing. Unlike Anthropic's widely released Opus and Sonnet models, Mythos Preview was never made generally available — Anthropic explicitly stated it did not plan to release it to the public, and distributed it only to vetted partners.

Mythos Preview is a broadly strong model that proved strikingly capable at computer-security tasks. Working through Project Glasswing, Anthropic and its partners used Claude Mythos Preview to find more than ten thousand high- or critical-severity vulnerabilities across systemically important software, including zero-day flaws in every major operating system and every major web browser. Anthropic notes these cybersecurity abilities emerged as a byproduct of improvements to coding and reasoning rather than from training the model specifically for vulnerability discovery.

Claude Mythos Preview was succeeded on June 9, 2026 by Claude Mythos 5 (the same restricted Project Glasswing distribution) and Claude Fable 5 (the publicly available variant with extra safety classifiers). Anthropic says users find Mythos 5 comparable to — or somewhat stronger than — Mythos Preview in most cases, while costing substantially less.

// FAQ

Is Claude Mythos Preview available to the public?

No. Anthropic stated it did not plan to make Claude Mythos Preview generally available. Access was invitation-only through Project Glasswing — initially around 40-50 partners (including Microsoft, Apple, Google, AWS, the Linux Foundation, Cisco, and Nvidia), later expanded to roughly 150-200 organizations across more than 15 countries.

What is Claude Mythos Preview used for?

It is a general-purpose model that proved exceptionally capable at cybersecurity. Project Glasswing partners used it defensively to scan critical codebases, where it found more than ten thousand high- or critical-severity vulnerabilities, including zero-days in every major operating system and web browser.

How does Mythos Preview relate to Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5?

Mythos Preview is the first model in Anthropic's Mythos-class line. It was succeeded on June 9, 2026 by Mythos 5 (still restricted to Project Glasswing) and Fable 5 (the public variant with additional safety classifiers). Anthropic says Mythos 5 is comparable to or somewhat stronger than Mythos Preview, at substantially lower cost.

How much did Claude Mythos Preview cost?

Anthropic quoted $25 per million input tokens and $125 per million output tokens for access following the research-preview period, available via the Claude API, Amazon Bedrock, Google Vertex AI, and Microsoft Foundry. The later Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models are priced lower at $10/$50 per million tokens.
