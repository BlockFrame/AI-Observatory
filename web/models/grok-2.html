Grok 2

business Maker: xAI (Grok) account_tree Family: Grok 2

// Overview

Grok 2 is xAI's flagship large language model from 2024, released in beta on August 13 and broadly to X Premium and Premium+ subscribers around August 20, 2024. It shipped alongside a lighter Grok 2 mini and was xAI's first model to add vision and, on X, image generation (initially via Black Forest Labs' FLUX.1). An early checkpoint tested anonymously as "sus-column-r" on the LMSYS Chatbot Arena leaderboard, where xAI said it outranked Claude 3.5 Sonnet and GPT-4-Turbo by Elo at the time.

On xAI's published academic benchmarks, Grok 2 scored 87.5% on MMLU, 75.5% on MMLU-Pro, 56.0% on GPQA, 76.1% on MATH and 88.4% on HumanEval, plus strong multimodal results of 66.1% on MMMU, 69.0% on MathVista and 93.6% on DocVQA. In December 2024 xAI brought the line to its enterprise API as grok-2-1212 (and grok-2-vision-1212) with a 131,072-token context window, priced at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens.

Grok 2 is a sparse mixture-of-experts transformer: roughly 270 billion total parameters across 64 blocks with 8 experts, of which 2 activate per token, for about 115 billion active parameters. In August 2025 xAI open-sourced the Grok 2.5 weights on Hugging Face under the source-available Grok 2 Community License Agreement (which forbids using the model to train competing models). Grok 2 is now a legacy model, superseded by Grok 3 and the Grok 4 family.

// FAQ

When was Grok 2 released?

xAI announced Grok 2 (and Grok 2 mini) in beta on August 13, 2024 and rolled it out to X Premium and Premium+ users around August 20, 2024. The grok-2-1212 API version followed in December 2024.

How big is Grok 2 and what is its architecture?

Grok 2 is a sparse mixture-of-experts transformer with roughly 270 billion total parameters across 64 blocks and 8 experts; only 2 experts fire per token, so about 115 billion parameters are active per forward pass.

How much does the Grok 2 API cost?

When xAI added grok-2-1212 and grok-2-vision-1212 to its enterprise API in December 2024, pricing was $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with a 131,072-token context window.

Are Grok 2's weights open?

Yes. In August 2025 xAI open-sourced the Grok 2.5 weights on Hugging Face under the source-available Grok 2 Community License Agreement, which permits use and modification but forbids training competing models with it.
