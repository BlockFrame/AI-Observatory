Claude Mythos 5

business Maker: Anthropic account_tree Family: Claude Fable / Mythos

// Overview

Claude Mythos 5 is Anthropic's top-tier, Mythos-class frontier model, announced on June 9, 2026 alongside its public twin, Claude Fable 5. The two share the same underlying model and the same specs and pricing; the difference is safety scope. Claude Fable 5 ships with blocking safety classifiers that decline or fall back to Claude Opus 4.8 in sensitive domains (cybersecurity, biology, chemistry, model distillation), while Claude Mythos 5 carries those classifiers removed. Because of that, Mythos 5 is not generally available — it is offered only in limited release to approved customers through Anthropic's Project Glasswing, the company's program for hardening critical software, working with the US government and partners such as AWS, Apple, Cisco, CrowdStrike, Google, Microsoft, NVIDIA and Palo Alto Networks.

Under the hood, Claude Mythos 5 carries a 1M-token context window, up to 128K output tokens per request, and a January 2026 knowledge cutoff. It accepts text and image input (including diagrams, charts and tables nested in PDFs) and returns text. It is the successor to the invitation-only Claude Mythos Preview, which Anthropic credits with finding thousands of high-severity software vulnerabilities, and Anthropic describes the Mythos-class line as the strongest cybersecurity model in the world.

Claude Mythos 5 reaches the API as model ID claude-mythos-5, billed at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens — about double Claude Opus 4.8 and roughly half the price of Mythos Preview. Days after launch, on June 12, 2026, Anthropic suspended access to both Claude Mythos 5 and Claude Fable 5 following a US export-control directive on access by foreign nationals. Anthropic announced on June 30, 2026 that the export controls had been lifted; Mythos 5 access was restored to a set of US organizations on June 26, 2026, with expansion to broader domestic and international Project Glasswing partners still being coordinated with the US government.

// FAQ

What is Claude Mythos 5?

Claude Mythos 5 is Anthropic's top-tier, Mythos-class frontier model, announced on June 9, 2026. It is the same underlying model as the public Claude Fable 5 but with Fable's blocking safety classifiers removed, and it is offered only in limited release to approved Project Glasswing partners rather than generally available.

How is Claude Mythos 5 different from Claude Fable 5?

They share the same model, specs and pricing. Claude Fable 5 includes safety classifiers that can decline requests or fall back to Claude Opus 4.8 in sensitive domains; Claude Mythos 5 does not include those classifiers, which is why it is restricted to vetted Project Glasswing customers instead of being publicly available.

How much does Claude Mythos 5 cost?

It is priced the same as Claude Fable 5: $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens, with a $1/MTok cache-hit rate. Batch API pricing is $5/MTok input and $25/MTok output. Access requires Project Glasswing approval.

Can I use Claude Mythos 5 today?

Only through Anthropic's Project Glasswing program. On June 12, 2026 Anthropic suspended access to both Claude Mythos 5 and Claude Fable 5 following a US export-control directive; those controls were lifted on June 30, 2026 and Mythos 5 access was restored to a set of US organizations on June 26, with Anthropic continuing to coordinate with the US government to expand access to broader domestic and international Glasswing partners. Even before the suspension, Mythos 5 was never generally available — access has always been limited to approved Project Glasswing customers.
